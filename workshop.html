Learning objectives

By the end, you should be able to

  1. Construct a structured prompt that specifies learner phase, competency, time available and output format.
  2. Derive three specific learning objectives aligned to one NMC CBME competency, stating domain and level.
  3. Develop a 30-minute session plan that uses at least one active-learning strategy.
  4. Generate a fictional clinical trigger case appropriate to the learner phase.
  5. Draft three formative questions aligned to the objectives, with answer keys.
  6. Verify AI-generated content against an authoritative source using a checklist, and write an AI-use disclosure statement.

What you leave with

A one-topic teaching pack: three objectives, a 30-minute session plan, a trigger case, three formative questions with keys, a completed verification checklist and an AI-use disclosure line.

Plus the workbook, six prompt cards, the 10-point verification checklist and an editable pack template.

A participation certificate is issued when you submit your teaching pack within 72 hours of the workshop.
